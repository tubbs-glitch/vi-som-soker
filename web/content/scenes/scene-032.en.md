You are in.

There is no direction. There is light but no source. There are no shadows either. You set down your foot and you see your foot and you do not see its shadow.

You breathe in. The air has no temperature. It is not cold and not warm. It is not air, perhaps, but you are breathing it.

You stand. You stand. It is not dark. It is grey.

You turn your head. The grey does not follow. The grey follows nothing.

You look down. You have your hands. You have your hands.

You look up. There is no up. You look in the direction you think your head is pointing and there is nothing there.

You hear nothing.

Then you hear.

It is not sound. It is something beginning.
