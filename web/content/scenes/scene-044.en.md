You have played *Vi som söker* — *We Who Search*.

This was your ending.

There are four others. Some are open to you now. Some you have already closed by choices you made in the hall, at the cassette player, on the bog. You made them as you made them. That was not wrong.

If you want, you can begin again.

You will remember things you were not meant to remember the second time — a code in a margin, a woman in a grey cardigan who does not turn, a gravestone six hundred metres out — and you will know things the one you were at the start did not. That is what replays are. It is not unfair. It is going back into a house you now know is a house.

The dairy is still there. It stood there in 1873, it stood there in 1981, it stood there when you turned in onto the drive. It will be standing when you close the page.

The frost gives. The frost takes.

We who search.

Thank you for reading.
