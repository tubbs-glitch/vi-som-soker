You leave Stockholm in the half-hour when the city does not know whether it is night or morning. At Värtahamnen three lorries stand with their headlamps off. On the E4 you are the only car for kilometres at a time.

In Uppland the light starts to come up. It is not dawn — only that the night never quite finished. The birch wood along the road is wet with dew though it has not rained. You drink your first coffee at a petrol station outside Gävle and you sit in the car while you drink it. It is still too early to make conversation with a till.

Gästrikland goes by. You shift down where the hills begin. The traffic thickens as you near Söderhamn and then thins again.

You have P1 on. A woman with a calm voice is talking about something you are not listening to. Outside Bollnäs the signal starts to crackle. You find a local station from Hudiksvall — a man speaking about electric cars and a bridge they are going to build. The station drops out somewhere north-west of Ljusdal, and you drive in silence for forty minutes before you have the energy to find another.

The forest changes. You do not always notice when it happens. One bend, one rise, and then there is spruce on both sides with pine in between. The ground is drier. You cross into Härjedalen without seeing the sign.

In Sveg you leave the E45 and turn onto Route 504, towards Lillhärdal. It is four in the afternoon. The sun is high and the gnats begin to hit the windscreen.
